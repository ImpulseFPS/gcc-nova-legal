GCC Nova – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains how GCC Nova, operated by Golden Cookie Club (TikTok: @goldencookieclub), handles information.

1. What data we collect

  • Basic TikTok profile information provided through TikTok Login Kit when you authorize the app (for example your open ID, display name and avatar, under the user.info.basic permission).
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok so the app can act on your behalf within the permissions you granted.
  • Video content you choose to upload, together with its caption, sent to your TikTok account as a draft (video.upload permission).

We do not collect your TikTok password, contacts, messages or payment information.

2. How we use it

The data is used only to provide the app's features: signing you in with TikTok and uploading the videos you select to your own TikTok account as drafts. Tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer and are not shared.

3. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Data is shared only with TikTok as needed to perform the upload you requested.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed to provide the service. You can revoke GCC Nova's access at any time in your TikTok app settings (Security / Manage app permissions), and you can ask us to delete any stored data by contacting us below.

5. Children

GCC Nova is an operator tool for the Golden Cookie Club account and is not directed at children; it is not intended to collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: goldencookieclubtv@gmail.com.
